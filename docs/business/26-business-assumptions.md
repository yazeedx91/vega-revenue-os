## Assumption Management

- Each assumption has an owner and a validation method
- No assumption is presented as a fact in business specifications
- Confirmed assumptions become constraints or decisions
- Disproven assumptions trigger business decision records

## Missing Product Constitution

The Product Constitution is an unresolved dependency. Until it is available, business assumptions are anchored to accepted/proposed ADRs and the Phase 02 specification.
