# KPI Framework

## North Star Metrics

The primary North Star metrics are business outcomes, not activity:

- **Qualified Meetings Booked**
- **Qualified Opportunities Created**
- **Qualified Pipeline Generated**
- **Revenue Influenced or Generated**

## KPI Hierarchy

| Category | Metric | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Business | Qualified meetings | Primary outcome |
| Business | Qualified opportunities | Pipeline quality |
| Business | Pipeline generated | Financial value |
| Business | Revenue influenced | Business impact |
| Efficiency | Cost per qualified meeting | Unit economics |
| Efficiency | Cost per opportunity | Sales efficiency |
| AI | AI operating cost | AI spend control |
| AI | Human intervention rate | Autonomy quality |
| AI | Autonomous completion rate | AI effectiveness |
| AI | AI accuracy | Output quality |
| Research | Research accuracy | Data quality |
| Outreach | Personalization quality | Message relevance |
| Conversation | Reply quality | Engagement quality |
| Meeting | Meeting quality | Prospect fit |
| Mission | Mission success rate | Overall mission performance |

## Not Vanity Metrics

The following are not North Star metrics and should not be used to measure success:

- Emails sent
- Contacts found
- Raw leads
- Page views
- Messages delivered
- Total calls made

## Measurement Cadence

| Metric | Frequency | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| North Star | Weekly / Monthly | Revenue Manager |
| AI efficiency | Daily / Weekly | RevOps |
| Research quality | Weekly | AI Governance |
| Mission success | Per mission / Monthly | Revenue Manager |
| Compliance | Continuous | Compliance Admin |

## KPI Definitions

- **Qualified Meeting**: A meeting with a prospect that meets the customer's qualification criteria and has been accepted by the prospect
- **Qualified Opportunity**: A CRM opportunity with a qualified prospect, a linked meeting, and explicit buying interest
- **Cost per Qualified Meeting**: Total AI and platform cost divided by qualified meetings in a period
- **Human Intervention Rate**: Percentage of AI actions that required human intervention
- **Autonomous Completion Rate**: Percentage of missions completed without human approval per action
- **AI Accuracy**: Percentage of AI outputs that are factually correct and aligned with policy
- **Research Accuracy**: Percentage of research facts that are source-backed and correct
- **Personalization Quality**: Human or LLM-evaluated relevance of outreach to the prospect
- **Reply Quality**: Whether replies are coherent, relevant, and aligned with customer policies
- **Meeting Quality**: Post-meeting qualification and fit rating
- **Mission Success Rate**: Percentage of missions meeting or exceeding their success criteria
