# Target Markets

## Primary Target Customer

The platform is sold to organizations that sell Microsoft Dynamics 365 or related services:

- Microsoft Dynamics 365 implementation partners
- Microsoft Dynamics consulting companies
- Dynamics 365 resellers
- Dynamics 365 service providers
- Dynamics-focused system integrators
- Organizations selling Dynamics 365 implementation, customization, migration, support, or Power Platform services

## Secondary Target Customer

- ERP implementation firms expanding into Dynamics 365
- Microsoft partners looking for an AI-native revenue offering
- Managed services providers with a Dynamics practice

## Future Target Customer

- Salesforce, NetSuite, SAP, or Oracle ERP partners once provider abstraction supports additional adapters
- Mid-market sales agencies and B2B service firms outside ERP
- SaaS companies with complex, consultative sales motions

## Targeted Prospect

The AI discovers and engages end-user organizations that may need Microsoft Dynamics 365 or related services. Initial example configuration targets:

- Manufacturing companies
- Logistics and distribution companies
- Retail companies

These are the prospects of the paying customer, not the primary platform users.

## Customer vs Prospect Distinction

| | Customer | Prospect |
|---|---|---|
| Pays for platform | Yes | No |
| Configures AI | Yes | No |
| Receives AI outreach | No | Yes |
| Is a lead in CRM | No | Yes |
| May become a lead for | The customer | The customer |

## Initial Geography

- **Initial example**: Saudi Arabia
- **Architecture constraint**: The ICP and territory are fully configurable; the system is not hardcoded to Saudi Arabia or any single country.
