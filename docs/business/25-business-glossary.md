# Business Glossary

## Account

A company or organization that is being tracked or engaged.

## Agent

An AI component that performs a specific business function within the revenue employee.

## Autonomy

The degree to which the AI may act without human approval.

## Buying Signal

An observable indicator that an account may have a need or interest in the customer's offering.

## Company

A provider-neutral domain concept representing an organization in the system.

## Contact

A provider-neutral domain concept representing a person associated with a company.

## Conversation

A thread of messages or interactions between the AI and a prospect.

## Engagement

Any interaction initiated by or received from a prospect.

## Human Approval

Explicit authorization from a human user before an AI performs an action.

## ICP (Ideal Customer Profile)

A configurable description of the companies and contacts that a customer wants to target.

## Intent

The inferred likelihood that an account or contact is interested in the customer's offering.

## Lead

A contact or account that has been identified and may be engaged.

## Meeting

A scheduled synchronous interaction between the customer's sales team and a prospect.

## Mission

A scoped, measurable, time-bound business task assigned to the AI employee.

## Opportunity

A provider-neutral domain concept representing a potential deal.

## Pipeline

The total value of opportunities at various stages.

## Prospect

An account or contact that is being targeted by the AI but has not yet become a customer.

## Qualified Lead

A lead that meets the customer-defined qualification criteria.

## Qualified Meeting

A scheduled meeting with a prospect that meets the customer's qualification criteria.

## Reply Quality

A measure of how relevant, coherent, and appropriate the AI's response is to a prospect message.

## Revenue

Income generated from closed deals.

## Signal

A piece of evidence that affects the scoring or qualification of an account or contact.

## Target Contact

A relevant decision-maker or influencer at a target account.
