# User Journeys

## Administrator

1. Log in to tenant
2. Configure provider credentials (Dynamics, Graph, Zoom)
3. Define user roles and permissions
4. Set compliance and retention policies
5. Review audit and integration health

## Revenue Manager

1. Create a mission from a business objective
2. Configure or refine the ICP
3. Review AI research and scoring
4. Approve or reject high-risk outreach
5. Monitor mission status and performance
6. Review performance and suggest improvements

## Sales Manager

1. Review assigned missions and prospects
2. Approve target contact lists
3. Review meeting briefs
4. Coach reps on qualified conversations
5. Review pipeline and conversion

## Sales Representative

1. Receive meeting brief
2. Join scheduled meeting
3. Record outcome in CRM
4. Provide feedback on lead quality
5. Request AI follow-up or new research

## Executive

1. View North Star dashboard
2. Review mission outcomes and ROI
3. Set strategic objectives
4. Approve high-value or high-risk initiatives

## Compliance Administrator

1. Review consent and opt-out lists
2. Audit AI communications
3. Investigate policy violations
4. Suspend or adjust missions
5. Generate compliance reports

## Common Journey Scenarios

### Creating a Mission

Objective → ICP selection → constraints → autonomy level → approval → launch

### Configuring ICP

Select filters → add signals → set thresholds → test on sample companies → save

### Reviewing AI Research

Open company record → view research summary → check sources → adjust score if needed

### Approving Outreach

Open approval queue → read draft → view research → approve, edit, or reject

### Handling Escalations

Receive escalation → review context and AI uncertainty → decide next step → record outcome

### Reviewing AI Performance

Open performance dashboard → compare missions → identify trends → adjust ICP or messaging
