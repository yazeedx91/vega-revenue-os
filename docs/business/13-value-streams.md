# Value Streams

## Primary Value Streams

### Prospect Discovery → Revenue

- Find companies matching the ICP
- Filter, score, and prioritize
- Engage relevant prospects
- Convert engagement into meetings
- Convert meetings into opportunities
- Convert opportunities into revenue

### Research → Intelligence

- Gather public and proprietary data
- Synthesize company and contact intelligence
- Identify buying signals
- Validate evidence and confidence

### Intelligence → Qualification

- Combine research, signals, and conversation data
- Score company and opportunity fit
- Disqualify poor-fit accounts
- Qualify high-fit accounts for outreach

### Qualification → Meeting

- Engage qualified prospects
- Manage replies and objections
- Confirm interest and need
- Schedule a meeting with the right stakeholders

### Meeting → Opportunity

- Conduct or support the meeting
- Record outcome and feedback
- Create CRM opportunity
- Prepare sales handoff

### Opportunity → Revenue

- Sales team progresses the opportunity
- AI supports follow-up and nurturing
- Opportunity wins or loses
- Outcome feeds back into learning

### Outcome → Learning

- Record mission and conversation outcomes
- Analyze what worked and what did not
- Refine ICP, messaging, and scoring
- Improve future mission performance

## Value Flow Principles

- Value is created at each stage by increasing certainty and readiness
- Low-quality data early reduces value downstream
- Human oversight adds value at high-risk points
- Learning loops compound value over time

## Value Measurement

| Value Stream | Key Metric |
|---|---|
| Discovery → Revenue | Qualified pipeline per mission |
| Research → Intelligence | Research confidence and accuracy |
| Intelligence → Qualification | Qualification rate |
| Qualification → Meeting | Meeting booking rate |
| Meeting → Opportunity | Opportunity conversion rate |
| Opportunity → Revenue | Revenue influenced |
| Outcome → Learning | Mission success rate improvement |
