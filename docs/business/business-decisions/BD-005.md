# BD-005: Human Oversight and Autonomy Level Definitions

- **Decision ID:** BD-005
- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-08-08
- **Owner:** Business Architecture Board

## Decision

Autonomy is configurable from Level 0 (human only) to Level 5 (fully autonomous within policy). High-risk, destructive, and contractual actions require human approval regardless of level.

## Context

Customers need control over how much the AI acts on its own. At the same time, safety and compliance require guardrails.

## Alternatives

1. Always require human approval
2. Always allow full autonomy
3. Fixed autonomy per tenant

## Rationale

Configurable levels let customers balance speed and control. Hard guardrails protect against unsafe actions.

## Impact

- Defines user permissions and approval workflows
- Drives process design for outreach, scheduling, and CRM creation
- Influences compliance and audit requirements
