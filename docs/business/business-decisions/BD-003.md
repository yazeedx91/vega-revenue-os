# BD-003: Fully Configurable ICP Framework

- **Decision ID:** BD-003
- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-08-08
- **Owner:** Business Architecture Board

## Decision

The ICP is fully configurable per tenant. No hardcoded ICP exists in the business architecture. Saudi manufacturing is only an initial example target market configuration.

## Context

Different customers will target different industries, geographies, and signals. A hardcoded ICP would limit the platform's addressable market.

## Alternatives

1. Hardcode an ICP for the initial launch
2. Provide only a few templates

## Rationale

A configurable ICP with hard filters, soft criteria, signals, evidence, and confidence thresholds allows each customer to define their own target market.

## Impact

- Requires flexible ICP data model
- Influences research, scoring, and discovery processes
- Requires confidence thresholds and evidence tracking
