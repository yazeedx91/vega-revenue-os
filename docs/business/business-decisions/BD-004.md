# BD-004: Commercial Model as Assumption Set

- **Decision ID:** BD-004
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Date:** 2026-08-08
- **Owner:** Business Architecture Board / Finance

## Decision

All pricing, margins, CAC, LTV, and commercial numbers are treated as assumptions requiring validation. A hybrid commercial model (base subscription + AI credits + outcome-linked fees) is the leading hypothesis but is not decided.

## Context

No validated market data is available. Selecting a model prematurely could create architectural and commercial lock-in.

## Alternatives

1. Pure subscription
2. Pure usage
3. Per-meeting
4. Per-opportunity
5. Hybrid

## Rationale

A hybrid model best aligns the platform with customer value and platform cost, but the exact dimensions must be validated with early customers.

## Impact

- Billing and metering architecture must support multiple dimensions
- Pricing must not be presented as fact in customer-facing materials
- Business model remains flexible

## Validation Needed

- Customer willingness-to-pay interviews
- Pilot pricing experiments
- Unit economics modeling
