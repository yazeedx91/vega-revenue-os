# BD-001: Paying Customer vs Targeted Prospect

- **Decision ID:** BD-001
- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-08-08
- **Owner:** Business Architecture Board

## Decision

The platform is sold to Microsoft Dynamics 365 implementation partners, consultancies, resellers, and service providers. The AI discovers and engages end-user organizations on behalf of these customers. The paying customer and the targeted prospect are distinct business actors.

## Context

A common risk in AI revenue tools is conflating the user of the platform with the target of the AI. This decision separates the two so the business model, ICP, and journeys are correctly defined.

## Alternatives

1. Sell directly to end-user organizations and have them use the AI themselves
2. Conflate customer and prospect into one actor
3. Use a platform marketplace model

## Rationale

The initial commercial specialization is selling autonomous pipeline generation to Dynamics 365 partners. The AI is the customer's employee, not a public tool for any company to self-prospect.

## Impact

- Affects business model, user personas, customer journeys, and ICP design
- Requires clear tenant-scoping per customer
- Influences integration, billing, and compliance design
