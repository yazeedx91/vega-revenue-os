# BD-006: Missing Product Constitution Dependency

- **Decision ID:** BD-006
- **Status:** Open
- **Date:** 2026-08-08
- **Owner:** Business Architecture Board

## Decision

The Product Constitution was not found in the expected repository locations. Business architecture proceeds without it but documents this as an unresolved dependency.

## Context

The Product Constitution was intended to be the authoritative source for project-wide principles. Its absence limits the ability to validate high-level product principles.

## Alternatives

1. Invent a Product Constitution
2. Use the Phase 02 specification as substitute
3. Proceed with existing ADRs and clearly mark the gap

## Rationale

The instruction is not to invent or create the Product Constitution during Phase 02. The existing ADRs and the Phase 02 specification provide enough direction to proceed, provided the gap is documented.

## Impact

- Business architecture may need revision once the constitution is available
- Project-wide principles are not claimed in business documents
- Conflicts between future constitution and current business architecture must be reconciled

## Validation Needed

- Locate or receive the Product Constitution
- Reconcile any contradictions with business architecture
