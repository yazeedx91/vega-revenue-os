# BD-002: Provider-Neutral Domain with Dynamics 365 First Specialization

- **Decision ID:** BD-002
- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-08-08
- **Owner:** Business Architecture Board

## Decision

Business domain concepts (Company, Contact, Lead, Opportunity, Activity, Meeting, Mission, ICP, Buying Signal) remain generic and provider-neutral. Microsoft Dynamics 365 is the first commercial specialization and integration, implemented in the adapter layer.

## Context

The product must not become hardcoded to a single CRM provider if it is to expand to other ERP/CRM specializations in the future.

## Alternatives

1. Hardcode all business concepts around Dynamics 365
2. Build a separate domain per provider

## Rationale

Provider-neutral concepts keep the core AI and business logic portable. Provider-specific behavior is isolated to adapters and specialization modules.

## Impact

- Enables future provider specializations
- Keeps AI prompts and reasoning provider-agnostic
- Requires adapter mapping and domain object normalization
