# Sales Model

## Sales Funnel

```
Discovered
   ↓
Researched
   ↓
Qualified Account
   ↓
Target Contact
   ↓
Outreach
   ↓
Engaged
   ↓
MQL/BQL
   ↓
SQL
   ↓
Meeting Requested
   ↓
Meeting Booked
   ↓
Opportunity
   ↓
Won
   ↓
Lost
   ↓
Disqualified
```

## Funnel Stage Definitions

| Stage | Definition |
|---|---|
| Discovered | Company matches ICP and is found in a source |
| Researched | Company has been researched and has an intelligence report |
| Qualified Account | Company meets hard filters and qualification score |
| Target Contact | Relevant decision-maker or influencer identified |
| Outreach | Personalized message has been sent or drafted |
| Engaged | Prospect has replied or interacted |
| MQL/BQL | Marketing or business qualified; shows interest and fit |
| SQL | Sales qualified; meets BANT or customer-defined criteria |
| Meeting Requested | Prospect has agreed to a meeting |
| Meeting Booked | Calendar invite sent and accepted |
| Opportunity | CRM opportunity created or updated |
| Won | Deal closed successfully |
| Lost | Deal closed unsuccessfully |
| Disqualified | Removed from funnel with reason |

## What Is NOT Measured

Vanity metrics are not treated as North Star metrics:

- Number of emails sent
- Number of contacts found
- Number of raw leads
- Number of messages delivered

## North Star Metrics

- Qualified meetings
- Qualified opportunities
- Pipeline generated
- Pipeline conversion
- Revenue influenced or generated
- Cost per qualified meeting

## Funnel Conversion Principles

- Each stage has clear, customer-configurable exit criteria
- The AI optimizes for quality, not volume
- Disqualification is a valid and measured outcome
- Conversion is tracked by mission and by ICP

## Sales Handoff

- When a meeting is booked, a sales brief is prepared
- The CRM opportunity is created or updated
- The assigned rep receives a notification and the brief
- The AI may support follow-up but does not own the close
