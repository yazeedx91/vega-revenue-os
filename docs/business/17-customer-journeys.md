# Customer Journeys

## Journey A: Buying the AI Platform

```
Discovery
   ↓
Evaluation
   ↓
Trial / Demonstration
   ↓
Security Review
   ↓
Procurement
   ↓
Implementation
   ↓
Configuration
   ↓
Activation
   ↓
First Mission
   ↓
First Qualified Meeting
   ↓
Expansion
   ↓
Renewal
```

### Stages

- **Discovery**: Partner learns about the AI employee offering
- **Evaluation**: Assesses fit for their business and ICP
- **Trial/Demonstration**: Sees a sample mission or pilot
- **Security Review**: Reviews security, compliance, and data handling
- **Procurement**: Negotiates contract and pricing
- **Implementation**: Tenant provisioned and integrations connected
- **Configuration**: ICP, messaging, autonomy, and policies set
- **Activation**: First mission configured and approved
- **First Mission**: AI runs an end-to-end mission
- **First Qualified Meeting**: First booked meeting delivered
- **Expansion**: More missions, users, or specializations
- **Renewal**: Contract renews based on value realized

## Journey B: Company Targeted by the AI

```
Discovered
   ↓
Researched
   ↓
Engaged
   ↓
Qualified
   ↓
Meeting Booked
   ↓
Sales Conversation
   ↓
Opportunity
   ↓
Won / Lost / Disqualified
```

### Stages

- **Discovered**: AI identifies the company as ICP-fit
- **Researched**: AI gathers context and signals
- **Engaged**: AI sends personalized outreach
- **Qualified**: Company expresses interest and meets criteria
- **Meeting Booked**: Prospect agrees to a meeting with the customer's sales team
- **Sales Conversation**: Customer's rep and prospect discuss needs
- **Opportunity**: Potential deal is formally tracked
- **Won/Lost/Disqualified**: Final outcome

## Journey C: Internal User Onboarding

1. Administrator creates tenant and configures integrations
2. Revenue Manager sets ICP and creates first mission
3. Sales Manager reviews approved missions and assigns reps
4. Sales Representative reviews meeting briefs and attends meetings
5. Compliance Administrator reviews policies and audit logs

## Journey Touchpoints

- Platform website and sales materials
- Sales demo and pilot mission
- Security questionnaire and review
- Configuration and onboarding calls
- Mission dashboard and approval inbox
- CRM updates and meeting briefs
- Performance reports and business reviews
