# Product Positioning

## Positioning Options Evaluated

| Option | Definition | Fit for This Product |
|--------|------------|----------------------|
| AI Lead Generator | Generates lists of leads | Too narrow; we do more than discovery |
| AI SDR | Automates outreach like a sales development rep | Close, but still transactional and not outcome-first |
| AI Sales Assistant | Helps human sellers | Too passive; our product acts autonomously |
| Sales Automation Platform | Automates sales tasks | Too generic; does not capture AI autonomy or outcomes |
| Revenue Intelligence Platform | Provides analytics and signals | Too analytical; our product executes, not only analyzes |
| Autonomous AI Sales Employee | Executes revenue missions with oversight | Accurate, but may be unfamiliar to buyers |
| AI-Native Revenue Operating System | System that plans, executes, and learns revenue work | Strong strategic positioning |

## Recommended Positioning

**Primary positioning**: AI-Native Autonomous Revenue Employee

**Supporting positioning**: AI-Native Revenue Operating System

## Why This Positioning

- **Autonomous Revenue Employee** directly describes what the product does: an AI that acts as an employee, not just a tool.
- **AI-Native** signals that autonomy, reasoning, learning, and multi-agent orchestration are core to the design, not afterthoughts.
- **Revenue Operating System** supports the broader strategic value for partners who want to systematize pipeline generation.
- The positioning matches the architecture (multi-agent, provider abstraction, mission model, human oversight) and the business outcome (qualified meetings and pipeline).

## What the Product Is Not

- Not a bulk email tool
- Not a static lead list provider
- Not a chatbot for websites
- Not a CRM replacement
- Not a marketing automation platform

## Positioning Implications

- Messaging should focus on outcomes: qualified meetings, pipeline, and booked meetings.
- Demos should show a mission from objective to booked meeting.
- Pricing conversations should tie to business value, not volume of emails or contacts.
