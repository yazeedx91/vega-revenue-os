# Success Metrics

## Business Success

- Qualified meetings per month
- Qualified opportunities per month
- Qualified pipeline generated
- Pipeline conversion rate
- Revenue influenced or generated
- Cost per qualified meeting
- Cost per qualified opportunity

## Product Success

- Mission success rate
- ICP score accuracy
- Outreach personalization quality
- Reply handling quality
- Meeting booking rate
- CRM opportunity creation rate
- User activation and retention

## AI Success

- AI accuracy on research and qualification
- Hallucination rate
- Confidence score calibration
- Autonomous completion rate
- Human intervention rate
- Model and prompt evaluation scores

## Operational Success

- Uptime and availability
- Integration health
- Time to first qualified meeting
- Exception and escalation rate
- Data quality scores
- Audit completeness

## Customer Success

- Customer activation time
- Time to first value
- Mission objective achievement
- Net Revenue Retention (assumption, to validate)
- Expansion rate
- Support ticket volume

## Financial Success

- MRR/ARR (assumption, to validate)
- Gross margin
- AI cost per mission
- CAC (assumption, to validate)
- LTV (assumption, to validate)
- Payback period (assumption, to validate)

## Security Success

- Security incidents
- Vulnerability remediation time
- Audit pass rate
- Consent and opt-out compliance
- Data retention compliance

## Reliability Success

- CRM sync success rate
- Meeting provider success rate
- Email/calendar provider success rate
- Mission completion without blocking
- Error recovery rate

## Measurement Criteria

All criteria are defined with:

- Clear definition
- Calculation method
- Data source
- Responsible owner
- Review cadence
- Target or threshold where known
