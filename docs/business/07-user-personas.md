# User Personas

## Platform Administrator

- **Responsibilities**: Tenant setup, user management, provider credentials, ICP configuration
- **Permissions**: Full tenant administration, billing, security settings
- **Goals**: Successful onboarding, stable operations, user adoption
- **Workflows**: Configure integrations, invite users, set ICP, review audit logs
- **Information needs**: Tenant health, user activity, integration status
- **AI interactions**: Configures AI behavior and autonomy levels

## Revenue Manager

- **Responsibilities**: Mission creation, ICP tuning, pipeline review, performance analysis
- **Permissions**: Create and manage missions, approve high-risk actions, view reports
- **Goals**: Hit revenue targets, improve AI performance, maintain quality
- **Workflows**: Create mission, review research, approve outreach, review meetings
- **Information needs**: Mission status, qualified meetings, conversion rates, AI accuracy
- **AI interactions**: Reviews and approves AI actions, provides feedback

## Sales Manager

- **Responsibilities**: Team management, opportunity review, coaching
- **Permissions**: View team pipeline, override AI actions, assign opportunities
- **Goals**: Team productivity, win rate, quota attainment
- **Workflows**: Review meetings, coach reps, monitor AI performance
- **Information needs**: Team activity, opportunity quality, win/loss data
- **AI interactions**: Receives AI-recommended actions and meeting briefs

## Sales Representative

- **Responsibilities**: Engage qualified prospects, close deals
- **Permissions**: View assigned opportunities, record outcomes, request AI tasks
- **Goals**: Meet quota, close deals, build relationships
- **Workflows**: Review meeting briefs, attend meetings, update CRM, provide feedback
- **Information needs**: Meeting briefs, prospect context, next-best actions
- **AI interactions**: Receives pre-qualified meetings and recommendations

## Business Development Representative

- **Responsibilities**: Early prospect engagement, qualification
- **Permissions**: View engaged prospects, respond on behalf of customer, request approvals
- **Goals**: Generate qualified meetings, maintain engagement quality
- **Workflows**: Review AI conversations, take over conversations, provide feedback
- **Information needs**: Conversation status, prospect signals, meeting bookings
- **AI interactions**: Collaborates with AI on conversation handling

## Executive

- **Responsibilities**: Strategic oversight, investment decisions
- **Permissions**: View dashboards and high-level reports
- **Goals**: Revenue growth, market expansion, ROI
- **Workflows**: Review performance dashboards, approve strategic missions
- **Information needs**: North Star metrics, pipeline, cost per outcome
- **AI interactions**: Sets objectives and reviews outcomes

## Compliance Administrator

- **Responsibilities**: Privacy, consent, opt-out, audit, policy enforcement
- **Permissions**: View compliance logs, enforce suppression, suspend missions
- **Goals**: Regulatory compliance, data protection, ethical AI use
- **Workflows**: Review opt-outs, audit AI communications, approve sensitive campaigns
- **Information needs**: Consent status, audit trail, policy violations
- **AI interactions**: Defines guardrails and reviews AI behavior
