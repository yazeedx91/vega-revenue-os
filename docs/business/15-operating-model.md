# Operating Model

## Customer Configuration

The customer operates the AI employee by configuring:

- **Objectives**: Business and revenue objectives
- **ICP**: Target company and contact criteria
- **Territories**: Geographies and regions
- **Industries**: Sectors and sub-sectors
- **Messaging rules**: Templates, tone, and channels
- **Autonomy level**: How much the AI may act on its own
- **Approval rules**: Which actions require human approval
- **Channels**: Email, LinkedIn, meeting providers
- **Working hours**: When outreach and scheduling may occur
- **Budgets**: AI execution and cost limits
- **Meeting availability**: Rep calendars and booking rules
- **CRM**: Provider credentials and synchronization rules
- **Escalation policies**: When and how to escalate
- **Compliance policies**: Consent, opt-out, retention, data handling

## Action Modes

| Mode | AI Role | Human Role |
|---|---|---|
| AI Executes | AI performs the action and logs it | Monitors and can override |
| AI Recommends | AI suggests action | Human decides and executes or delegates |
| AI Requests Approval | AI drafts and pauses for approval | Reviews, approves, edits, or rejects |
| Human Executes | Human performs action | AI may provide context and recommendations |

## Operating Rhythm

1. **Weekly**: Review mission performance, AI accuracy, and pipeline
2. **Daily**: Review approvals, escalations, and exceptions
3. **Continuous**: AI executes low-risk tasks within policy

## Governance Cadence

- **Mission launch**: Configure, approve, launch
- **Mission execution**: Monitor, approve, adjust
- **Mission close**: Review, learn, archive
- **Monthly business review**: KPIs, outcomes, strategy updates

## Human-in-the-Loop Points

- Mission approval
- High-risk outreach
- Sensitive industry or message
- Low-confidence AI output
- Meeting with high-value prospect
- CRM opportunity creation
- Strategy or ICP changes
- Compliance exceptions

## AI Autonomy Boundaries

- AI never acts outside configured policies
- AI never bypasses security or compliance
- AI never commits the customer to pricing or contracts
- AI always records and explains its actions
- AI escalates when uncertain or beyond authority
