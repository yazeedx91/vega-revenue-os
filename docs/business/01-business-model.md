# Business Model

## Who Pays

The primary paying customer is a Microsoft Dynamics 365 implementation partner, consultancy, reseller, or service provider. These organizations purchase the platform to deploy an AI-native autonomous revenue employee that can discover, research, engage, and qualify prospects on their behalf.

## Why They Pay

These organizations need a predictable, scalable source of qualified pipeline and booked meetings for Dynamics 365 services. They currently rely on manual prospecting, inconsistent outreach, and underutilized sales capacity.

## Problem Being Purchased

- Manual and time-consuming prospect research
- Inconsistent messaging and follow-up
- Difficulty identifying the right decision-makers
- Low conversion from lead to qualified meeting
- High cost of sales development headcount
- Limited visibility into pipeline quality

## Value Created

- **Immediate value**: automated research, personalized outreach, and meeting booking
- **Recurring value**: continuous learning, better targeting, and improved conversion over time
- **Financial outcomes**: more qualified meetings, larger qualified pipeline, shorter sales cycles, lower customer acquisition cost
- **Strategic value**: differentiated service offering, data-driven sales motion, and scalable revenue operations

## Difficulty to Replace

The platform becomes difficult to replace once it has accumulated:

- ICP tuning data for a specific market
- Conversation history and response patterns
- Integrated provider credentials and workflows
- Learned personalization and scoring models

## Customer Retention Drivers

- Measurable business outcomes (qualified meetings, pipeline)
- Continuous improvement of targeting and messaging
- Ease of configuration and oversight
- Multi-tenant data security and compliance

## Expansion Opportunities

- Additional user seats and teams
- Additional provider specializations (e.g., Salesforce, NetSuite)
- Additional geographies and industry verticals
- Advanced AI reasoning and workflow capabilities

## Recommended Commercial Model

### Hypothesis: Hybrid Usage + Outcome-Linked Pricing

- **Base platform subscription** per customer tenant for access, configuration, and oversight
- **AI execution credits** for autonomous actions (research, outreach, conversation)
- **Outcome-linked fees** for qualified meetings or qualified opportunities, subject to clear attribution

### Alternatives Analyzed

| Model | Pros | Cons | Status |
|-------|------|------|--------|
| Pure SaaS subscription | Predictable revenue | Misaligned with outcomes | Hypothesis |
| Usage-based | Scales with activity | Can encourage spam volume | Hypothesis |
| AI execution credits | Aligns cost with actions | Complex metering | Hypothesis |
| Per qualified meeting | Outcome-aligned | Attribution and quality disputes | Hypothesis |
| Per opportunity | Strong outcome link | Long sales cycles affect timing | Hypothesis |
| Per seat | Simple | Does not reflect AI value | Hypothesis |
