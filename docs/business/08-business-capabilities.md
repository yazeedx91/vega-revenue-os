# Business Capabilities

## Capability Map

### Mission & Strategy

- **Mission Management**: Create, plan, execute, monitor, and complete revenue missions
- **Objective Setting**: Translate business objectives into measurable missions
- **Strategy Improvement**: Learn from outcomes and refine targeting and messaging

### Market & Account Intelligence

- **Market Discovery**: Find companies matching the configured ICP
- **Company Intelligence**: Research and synthesize company context
- **Buying Signal Detection**: Identify signals of need or intent
- **Lead Intelligence**: Enrich and qualify leads

### Contact & Decision-Maker Intelligence

- **Contact Intelligence**: Find and enrich contact information
- **Decision-Maker Mapping**: Identify relevant decision-makers and influencers
- **Persona Modeling**: Understand roles, priorities, and communication preferences

### Outreach & Engagement

- **Personalization**: Generate tailored outreach based on research
- **Outreach**: Send and manage emails, LinkedIn messages, or other approved channels
- **Conversation Management**: Handle prospect replies and maintain context
- **Lead Qualification**: Assess prospect fit and interest

### Meeting & Pipeline

- **Meeting Scheduling**: Book meetings through Zoom or Google Meet
- **CRM Management**: Create and update Dynamics 365 opportunities
- **Opportunity Scoring**: Rank and prioritize opportunities
- **Sales Handoff**: Prepare and deliver briefs for sales reps

### AI & Governance

- **AI Agent Management**: Configure, monitor, and manage AI agents
- **AI Governance**: Enforce model, prompt, and output governance
- **Knowledge Management**: Maintain domain knowledge, templates, and learned patterns

### Operations & Compliance

- **Human Oversight**: Define and enforce approval and escalation policies
- **Compliance**: Manage consent, opt-out, data retention, and audit
- **Tenant Management**: Configure per-tenant settings and policies
- **User Management**: Manage users and roles
- **Billing**: Track usage and support invoicing
- **Analytics**: Measure outcomes and report KPIs
