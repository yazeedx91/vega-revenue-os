# Business Objectives

## Objective Hierarchy

```
Company Goal
      ↓
Revenue Objective
      ↓
Mission
      ↓
Business Outcome
      ↓
Tasks
```

## Company Goal

A long-term, strategic goal for the customer's business. Example: "Become the leading Dynamics 365 implementation partner in Saudi Arabia."

## Revenue Objective

A measurable revenue-related target that supports the company goal. Examples:

- "Generate 20 qualified meetings in 30 days."
- "Generate $2M qualified pipeline in Q3."
- "Acquire 10 new manufacturing customers this year."

## Mission

A concrete, scoped assignment given to the AI employee. Examples:

- "Find 100 high-fit manufacturing companies and book 20 qualified meetings."
- "Identify expansion opportunities in existing accounts."

Each mission has:

- Objective
- Target ICP
- Constraints
- Budget
- Timeframe
- Priority
- Success criteria

## Business Outcome

A measurable result of a mission. Examples:

- Number of qualified meetings booked
- Dollar value of qualified pipeline
- Number of qualified opportunities created
- Conversion rates

## Tasks

The individual actions the AI performs to achieve outcomes. Examples:

- Discover 50 companies
- Research a company
- Send personalized outreach
- Handle a reply
- Book a meeting

## Measurement

| Level | Example Metric |
|---|---|
| Company Goal | Market share, revenue growth |
| Revenue Objective | Qualified pipeline, meetings |
| Mission | Meetings booked, opportunities |
| Business Outcome | Meeting conversion, pipeline |
| Task | Outreach sent, reply rate |

## Measurement Principles

- Outcomes are measured, not just activity.
- Each objective has a target and a timeframe.
- Objectives are configured per tenant.
- AI performance is tracked against objective outcomes.
