# Buyer Personas

## Persona 1: CEO of a Dynamics 365 Partner

- **Goals**: Predictable revenue growth, scalable operations, competitive differentiation
- **Pain points**: Inconsistent pipeline, high cost of sales development, unpredictable revenue
- **Buying triggers**: Need to scale sales, pressure to improve ROI, new service launch
- **Objections**: Security, AI reliability, cost, team adoption
- **Success criteria**: More qualified meetings, measurable pipeline, lower CAC
- **Decision authority**: Economic buyer; signs contract
- **Economic influence**: High
- **Technical influence**: Low
- **Security concerns**: Data privacy, customer data handling, reputation
- **Expected ROI**: 3-5x return on investment within one year

## Persona 2: CRO / VP Sales

- **Goals**: Pipeline quality, forecast accuracy, rep productivity
- **Pain points**: Low lead quality, inaccurate forecasts, wasted rep time
- **Buying triggers**: Missed targets, expansion plans, new market entry
- **Objections**: Quality of AI conversations, human oversight
- **Success criteria**: Qualified meetings per month, conversion to opportunity
- **Decision authority**: Strong influencer; shapes requirements
- **Economic influence**: Medium-High
- **Technical influence**: Low
- **Security concerns**: Data access controls, auditability
- **Expected ROI**: Improved conversion and rep productivity

## Persona 3: Revenue Operations Director

- **Goals**: Process efficiency, data quality, measurable outcomes
- **Pain points**: Fragmented tools, inconsistent data, manual reporting
- **Buying triggers**: Tool consolidation, need for automation
- **Objections**: Integration complexity, data migration
- **Success criteria**: Clean data, clear KPIs, audit trail
- **Decision authority**: Influencer; evaluator
- **Economic influence**: Medium
- **Technical influence**: Medium
- **Security concerns**: Compliance, data retention, tenant isolation
- **Expected ROI**: Operational efficiency and reduced manual work

## Persona 4: IT Director

- **Goals**: Secure, compliant, maintainable technology
- **Pain points**: Shadow IT, integration risk, compliance gaps
- **Buying triggers**: New vendor evaluation, security review
- **Objections**: Data residency, AI security, vendor lock-in
- **Success criteria**: Security certification alignment, role-based access, audit logs
- **Decision authority**: Gatekeeper for security and procurement
- **Economic influence**: Low
- **Technical influence**: High
- **Security concerns**: Multi-tenancy, encryption, third-party integrations, consent
- **Expected ROI**: Reduced risk and integration burden
