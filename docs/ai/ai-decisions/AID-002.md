# AID-002: Structured Reasoning and Evidence for AI Decisions

- **Decision ID:** AID-002
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Date:** 2026-08-08
- **Owner:** Chief AI Architect

## Decision

Important AI decisions must be represented as structured records containing reasoning summary, evidence references, confidence, policy check, and rationale. Raw chain-of-thought is not exposed or stored as authoritative.

## Context

Auditable, explainable AI decisions are required for governance, compliance, debugging, and human oversight. Exposing raw chain-of-thought is not desirable for security, privacy, and clarity.

## Rationale

Structured reasoning artifacts provide the right level of transparency while limiting leakage of internal model reasoning. Evidence links make decisions verifiable.

## Impact

- Decision records become first-class domain artifacts.
- Reasoning artifacts feed audit, human approval, and evaluation.
- Downstream systems consume structured decisions, not raw LLM text.
