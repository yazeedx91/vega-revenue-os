# AID-005: Controlled AI Evaluation and Learning Loop

- **Decision ID:** AID-005
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Date:** 2026-08-08
- **Owner:** Chief AI Architect / AI Evaluation Specialist

## Decision

Learning from outcomes is controlled and governance-gated. The system does not autonomously retrain itself. Changes to prompts, agents, policies, and models are evaluated against golden datasets and business metrics before promotion.

## Context

Uncontrolled online learning can introduce drift, bias, policy violations, and unpredictable behavior in a multi-tenant revenue system.

## Rationale

Controlled evaluation ensures improvements are measurable, safe, and reversible. Human review remains for high-impact changes.

## Impact

- Evaluation loop is a defined architecture component.
- Golden datasets and business metrics drive promotion.
- Feature flags and versioning support A/B testing.
- No autonomous model retraining without explicit governance.
