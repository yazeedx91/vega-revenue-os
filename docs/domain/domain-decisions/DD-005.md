# DD-005: Lead as Separate Aggregate from Company and Contact

- **Decision ID:** DD-005
- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-08-08
- **Owner:** Domain Architecture Board

## Decision

`Lead` is a separate aggregate from `Company` and `Contact`. It references them by identity and has its own qualification lifecycle.

## Context

Company and Contact are intelligence/identity aggregates. Lead is a sales candidate with qualification state, disqualification reasons, and conversion rules.

## Alternatives

1. Lead as a value object inside Company or Contact
2. Lead as a status flag on Contact
3. Lead as a separate aggregate referencing Company and Contact

## Rationale

A lead has its own lifecycle, invariants, and audit needs. Combining it with Company or Contact would blur responsibilities and create a large aggregate.

## Impact

- Clear separation between data intelligence and sales qualification.
- Lead can be disqualified or converted without deleting Company/Contact.
- Qualification history belongs to the Lead aggregate.
