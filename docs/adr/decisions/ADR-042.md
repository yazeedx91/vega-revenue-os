# ADR-042: Integration Rate Limiting and Resilience

- **ADR ID:** ADR-042
- **Status:** Accepted
- **Group:** Integrations
- **Date:** 2026-08-08
- **Decision Owners:** Architecture Board, CTO

## Context

The AI-Native Autonomous Revenue Employee requires clear integrations direction so that implementation, operations, and future evolution remain coherent. This ADR captures the relevant drivers, alternatives, and consequences for the integration rate limiting and resilience concern.

## Problem

Multiple integrations options exist for integration rate limiting and resilience. Without a documented decision, individual teams may choose inconsistent technologies or patterns, creating integration debt, security gaps, and operational sprawl.

## Requirements

- Respect provider quotas
- recover from transient failures
- prevent abuse

## Decision

Apply per-tenant and per-provider rate limiting, retries with backoff, circuit breakers, and idempotent outbound calls.

## Alternatives

1. **No rate limits or resilience patterns**
2. **Simple global throttle**

## Rationale

External APIs have quotas and failures. Per-tenant and per-provider controls protect providers and prevent noisy-neighbor issues.

## Consequences

This decision aligns the integrations domain with the overall architecture. It enables consistency, reduces cross-cutting risk, and leaves room to revisit alternatives when validation data is available.

## Risks

- Retry storms
- false circuit breaker trips

## Mitigations

- Exponential backoff
- jitter
- health checks
- idempotency keys

## Security Impact

Provider adapters normalize external systems behind domain ports; credentials and tokens are stored in a secrets manager.

## Performance Impact

Caching, idempotency keys, and rate-limiting adapters protect downstream APIs from burst traffic.

## Scalability Impact

New CRM/ERP/email/calendar providers are added as additional adapters, not core changes.

## Cost Impact

Standardized adapters avoid bespoke integrations and enable reuse across tenants.

## Operational Impact

Adapter health and retry metrics are observable; failures are contained within the integration bounded context.

## AI Impact

The intelligence layer receives normalized domain objects, so prompts and planning logic remain provider-agnostic.

## Reversibility

Medium. The decision can be revisited when validation criteria or operational constraints change; alternatives are documented and can be re-evaluated.
