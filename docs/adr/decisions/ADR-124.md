# ADR-124: Dynamics 365 Read-Only in Phase 14

- **ADR ID:** ADR-124
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Group:** Intelligence / CRM Integration
- **Date:** 2026-08-13
- **Decision Owners:** Architecture Review Board, CTO

## Context

Phase 11 defined the `IDynamicsIntelligenceAdapter` port and a stub implementation for read-only duplicate detection. Phase 14 could extend this to write email activities back to Dynamics 365, but CRM write-back is a cross-cutting concern that couples multiple bounded contexts.

## Decision

1. Dynamics 365 remains **read-only / deferred** in Phase 14.
2. ProjectX is the system of record for:
   - Email send state and delivery state.
   - Replies and conversation state.
   - Outreach execution and sequence state.
   - Suppression state.
   - Email audit events.
3. Dynamics 365 may still be read for enrichment and duplicate detection through the existing port.
4. Future CRM synchronization must consume explicit ProjectX domain/integration events rather than directly coupling email execution to Dynamics writes.

## Alternatives

1. **Write email send/reply activities back to Dynamics in Phase 14.** Rejected: requires bidirectional sync, conflict resolution, and CRM error handling before the email channel itself is proven.
2. **Make Dynamics the system of record for email interactions.** Rejected: violates the bounded-context boundary and would force ProjectX to depend on CRM availability for core outreach state.
3. **Use a shared database/view with Dynamics.** Rejected: creates tight coupling and security concerns.

## Rationale

Deferring CRM write-back keeps Phase 14 focused on the email channel while establishing ProjectX as the authoritative source for email interactions. A future sync adapter can subscribe to stable events without retrofitting the execution path.

## Consequences

- `DynamicsIntelligenceAdapterStub` remains the only Dynamics adapter.
- No CRM write-back code is added to `OutreachExecutionService` or `ConversationHandlingService`.
- Email audit data lives in ProjectX until a future synchronization phase.
- Event schema stability becomes important for future CRM consumers.

## Addendum (2026-08-17, Phase 13.5)

See `ADR-125` for the canonical `IBusinessSystemConnector` abstraction and the rename of `IDynamicsIntelligenceAdapter` → `IBusinessSystemIntelligenceAdapter`. This ADR's decision (Dynamics 365 read-only/deferred in Phase 14, ProjectX as system of record for email interactions) is unchanged.
