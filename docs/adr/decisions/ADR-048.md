# ADR-048: Communication Personalization

- **ADR ID:** ADR-048
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Group:** Intelligence
- **Date:** 2026-08-08
- **Decision Owners:** Architecture Board, CTO

## Context

The AI-Native Autonomous Revenue Employee requires clear intelligence direction so that implementation, operations, and future evolution remain coherent. This ADR captures the relevant drivers, alternatives, and consequences for the communication personalization concern.

## Problem

Multiple intelligence options exist for communication personalization. Without a documented decision, individual teams may choose inconsistent technologies or patterns, creating integration debt, security gaps, and operational sprawl.

## Requirements

- Personalize messages
- maintain brand
- enforce approval gates

## Decision

Generate personalized communications using approved templates, profile data, and tone guidelines; high-impact sends require approval.

## Alternatives

1. **One generic message for all**
2. **Fully autonomous copy without oversight**

## Rationale

Template-based personalization keeps brand voice consistent and safe while allowing customization.

## Consequences

This decision aligns the intelligence domain with the overall architecture. It enables consistency, reduces cross-cutting risk, and leaves room to revisit alternatives when validation data is available.

## Risks

- Tone drift
- compliance
- off-brand

## Mitigations

- Template approval
- tone guardrails
- human review gate

## Security Impact

Personalization models operate on tenant-scoped data; PII is masked or tokenized before research or model calls.

## Performance Impact

Feature stores and cached embeddings let personalization and scoring run at sub-second latencies.

## Scalability Impact

Persona and signal data is partitioned by tenant; model scoring can be sharded horizontally.

## Cost Impact

Storing derived signals in PostgreSQL/Redis avoids re-running expensive LLM inferences for repeated queries.

## Operational Impact

Signals and recommendations are versioned and auditable, enabling debugging of customer-facing advice.

## AI Impact

Agentic intelligence is grounded in retrieval, constraints, and user feedback rather than unconstrained generation.

## Reversibility

High. The decision can be revisited when validation criteria or operational constraints change; alternatives are documented and can be re-evaluated.
