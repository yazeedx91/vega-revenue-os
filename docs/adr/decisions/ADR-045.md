# ADR-045: Persona and Preference Learning

- **ADR ID:** ADR-045
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Group:** Intelligence
- **Date:** 2026-08-08
- **Decision Owners:** Architecture Board, CTO

## Context

The AI-Native Autonomous Revenue Employee requires clear intelligence direction so that implementation, operations, and future evolution remain coherent. This ADR captures the relevant drivers, alternatives, and consequences for the persona and preference learning concern.

## Problem

Multiple intelligence options exist for persona and preference learning. Without a documented decision, individual teams may choose inconsistent technologies or patterns, creating integration debt, security gaps, and operational sprawl.

## Requirements

- Learn preferences
- avoid sensitive inference
- respect consent

## Decision

Capture explicit preferences and derive behavioral signals; do not infer sensitive or protected attributes.

## Alternatives

1. **Fully autonomous profile inference**
2. **Static personas without learning**

## Rationale

Explicit and behavioral signals balance personalization with privacy and fairness. Avoiding sensitive inferences reduces compliance and reputational risk.

## Consequences

This decision aligns the intelligence domain with the overall architecture. It enables consistency, reduces cross-cutting risk, and leaves room to revisit alternatives when validation data is available.

## Risks

- Privacy concerns
- stereotyping
- stale preferences

## Mitigations

- Consent
- transparency
- regular refresh
- bias review

## Security Impact

Personalization models operate on tenant-scoped data; PII is masked or tokenized before research or model calls.

## Performance Impact

Feature stores and cached embeddings let personalization and scoring run at sub-second latencies.

## Scalability Impact

Persona and signal data is partitioned by tenant; model scoring can be sharded horizontally.

## Cost Impact

Storing derived signals in PostgreSQL/Redis avoids re-running expensive LLM inferences for repeated queries.

## Operational Impact

Signals and recommendations are versioned and auditable, enabling debugging of customer-facing advice.

## AI Impact

Agentic intelligence is grounded in retrieval, constraints, and user feedback rather than unconstrained generation.

## Reversibility

High. The decision can be revisited when validation criteria or operational constraints change; alternatives are documented and can be re-evaluated.
