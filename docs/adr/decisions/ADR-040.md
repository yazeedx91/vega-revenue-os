# ADR-040: External Research and Web Crawling

- **ADR ID:** ADR-040
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Group:** Integrations
- **Date:** 2026-08-08
- **Decision Owners:** Architecture Board, CTO

## Context

The AI-Native Autonomous Revenue Employee requires clear integrations direction so that implementation, operations, and future evolution remain coherent. This ADR captures the relevant drivers, alternatives, and consequences for the external research and web crawling concern.

## Problem

Multiple integrations options exist for external research and web crawling. Without a documented decision, individual teams may choose inconsistent technologies or patterns, creating integration debt, security gaps, and operational sprawl.

## Requirements

- Research companies and contacts
- respect source limits
- avoid abuse

## Decision

Use a dedicated external research service with search APIs and controlled, rate-limited web crawling.

## Alternatives

1. **Rely on model pre-trained knowledge**
2. **Build and operate an own crawler cluster**

## Rationale

A dedicated service isolates research from core, respects rate limits, and keeps AI responses grounded in current sources.

## Consequences

This decision aligns the integrations domain with the overall architecture. It enables consistency, reduces cross-cutting risk, and leaves room to revisit alternatives when validation data is available.

## Risks

- Data quality
- legal crawling limits
- cost

## Mitigations

- Robots.txt
- rate limits
- source validation
- caching

## Security Impact

Provider adapters normalize external systems behind domain ports; credentials and tokens are stored in a secrets manager.

## Performance Impact

Caching, idempotency keys, and rate-limiting adapters protect downstream APIs from burst traffic.

## Scalability Impact

New CRM/ERP/email/calendar providers are added as additional adapters, not core changes.

## Cost Impact

Standardized adapters avoid bespoke integrations and enable reuse across tenants.

## Operational Impact

Adapter health and retry metrics are observable; failures are contained within the integration bounded context.

## AI Impact

The intelligence layer receives normalized domain objects, so prompts and planning logic remain provider-agnostic.

## Reversibility

High. The decision can be revisited when validation criteria or operational constraints change; alternatives are documented and can be re-evaluated.
